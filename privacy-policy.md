# Visponsa Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026  
**Publisher / data controller:** Cybapalace  
**Privacy contact:** chiakwak@outlook.com  


## 1. About Visponsa

Visponsa helps people reviewing LinkedIn job postings identify text relating to visa sponsorship, relocation opportunities and remote work. It displays matching text as evidence. Its local job-library features let you save job information, add notes, track application status, search and filter saved jobs, and export them as CSV files.

This policy covers the Visponsa browser extension distributed through any browser marketplace in which the publisher makes it available, including versions intended for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and Brave. Availability and technical compatibility may differ by browser. It does not describe the independent practices of browser vendors, marketplaces, LinkedIn or websites you choose to visit.

## 2. How job analysis works

When you view a supported LinkedIn job posting, Visponsa reads relevant content from that page inside your browser. This may include the job title, employer name, employer page identifier, job identifier, location, workplace type and job description.

The current version uses local phrase-matching rules to identify visa, relocation and remote-work signals. It does not send this job content to the publisher, an AI provider or an analytics service for analysis. The current version does not scan company websites or automatically fetch LinkedIn company pages.

Visponsa does not require access to your LinkedIn password and is not designed to read private messages, your connections list or unrelated browsing history. Because the extension can run on LinkedIn pages, its site-access permission is broader than an individual job URL; its intended analysis is limited to supported job postings you view.

## 3. Information stored in your browser

Depending on the features you use, Visponsa stores the following in the extension's local browser-profile storage:

- **Interface preferences:** for example, whether the job-analysis panel is collapsed.
- **Local Pro preview setting:** whether you have enabled the current free local preview of job-management features. This is not a payment record or verified subscription entitlement.
- **Jobs you choose to save:** job title, company name and identifier, location, workplace type, job description, job identifier, original listing link, and the visa and remote-work result labels associated with the saved job.
- **Your job-management information:** notes, application status, and dates when a job was saved or updated.

The current extension uses local storage rather than browser sync storage. It does not intentionally upload this information to a Visponsa server or synchronise it between your devices. Your browser, operating system, device administrator or backup provider may separately manage device or profile backups under their own policies.

If you used an earlier Visponsa preview that offered company scanning, previously saved company website addresses or scan results may remain in the same extension's local storage after an update. The current version does not use or display those scan results. You can remove legacy data by clearing the extension's local storage or uninstalling it; export any saved jobs you wish to keep first.

Avoid putting passwords, financial details or unnecessary sensitive personal information in your job notes.

## 4. Information the publisher receives

The current extension does not automatically send the publisher your job-page content, saved jobs, notes or application history. It does not include advertising trackers, analytics collection or automated error-reporting services, and it does not sell or rent your personal information.

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message and any attachments. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request. We use support information to respond, troubleshoot issues, maintain appropriate records and comply with applicable law. Because local extension data is not uploaded to us, we generally cannot retrieve or restore it for you.

A browser marketplace may give the publisher reviews, support communications, aggregate installation statistics, purchase-related information or other reports under that marketplace's own policies. Such reporting is separate from data collected by the extension itself. We use information made available to us only as necessary to operate, support and administer Visponsa and meet legal obligations.

## 5. External links and third-party services

Visponsa includes optional lookup links, including links to search engines, sponsorship-information services and public government resources. Some links contain the company name or other displayed lookup terms in their destination URL. When you choose to open a link, your browser requests that destination, and the third party may receive those terms, your IP address, browser information and other data under its own policies.

Opening an original job listing similarly takes you to LinkedIn. Visponsa does not control those sites or their data practices. Read their privacy policies before using them.

The current extension does not itself set advertising or tracking cookies. LinkedIn, browser marketplaces and external websites may use cookies independently.

## 6. Purchases and future subscriptions

The current local Pro preview is not a paid subscription and does not require a Visponsa account, payment card or licence server. The publisher may charge an upfront marketplace price or offer paid features in a future release, subject to the terms clearly disclosed at purchase.

If you buy Visponsa through a browser marketplace, that marketplace or its payment provider handles the purchase according to its own privacy and payment terms. Visponsa's current extension code does not collect or process your card details.

Before introducing accounts, direct checkout, subscription verification, cloud storage, remote analysis or other material changes to data processing, we will update this policy and provide any notices and choices required by applicable law. A future payment or subscription system may require additional disclosures identifying the relevant providers, information processed, purposes, retention and international transfers. This section does not authorise undisclosed future collection.

## 7. Permissions

The current extension requests permissions needed for its stated features:

- **Local storage:** to remember preferences and retain jobs and information you choose to save.
- **Active tab access:** to communicate with the supported LinkedIn tab when you open the toolbar popup.
- **LinkedIn site access:** to read supported job-page content and display the local analysis panel.

The current version does not request optional access to arbitrary company websites for scanning. Your browser's permission wording may differ. You can review, restrict or revoke permissions through its extension settings, although doing so may prevent features from working.

## 8. Sharing, sale and advertising

We do not sell local extension data, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with recruiters or employers. Saved jobs, notes and statuses are not sent to employers through Visponsa.

Information you deliberately send to support may be handled by the email or support providers we use to deliver that service. We may disclose information in our possession where required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect lawful rights and security. This does not give us access to data held only in your browser.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Local information remains in your browser profile until you remove it, clear extension storage or uninstall the extension, subject to your browser's behaviour. You can delete individual saved jobs through the job library. Turning off Pro preview hides its features but does not delete saved jobs.

CSV files you export are separate files stored wherever you save them. Deleting a saved job or uninstalling Visponsa does not delete those exports, browser backups or copies you have shared. Manage those copies separately.

Support correspondence and other information actually received by the publisher are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were received, applicable legal obligations and the resolution of disputes. Contact us to request deletion of information in our possession, subject to lawful retention requirements.

## 10. Security

Visponsa uses local processing to limit transmission of job content and job-library data. Local storage and CSV exports are not a secure vault and are not protected by a separate Visponsa password or an application-level encryption feature. Someone with sufficient access to your device, browser profile, exported files or backups may be able to access that information.

Keep your browser and device up to date, protect your account and device access, and store exports carefully. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose not to save jobs, edit or delete saved information, export your library, disable the local Pro preview, restrict site access, disable the extension or uninstall it.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights concerning personal information held by the publisher, including access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability, and the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority. Contact the privacy email above to exercise an applicable right. We may need to verify your request proportionately. We cannot remotely access or delete local browser data that has not been shared with us, but we can explain how you can manage it.

## 12. International processing

The extension's current job analysis and library storage take place in your browser rather than on a Visponsa server. External sites, marketplace operators and support-service providers may process information in other countries under their own arrangements and policies. If the publisher introduces international transfers of personal information under its control, it will provide the disclosures and safeguards required by applicable law.

## 13. Children

Visponsa is intended for people reviewing employment opportunities and is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where applicable law requires it. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can assess and address the request.

## 14. Accuracy and independence

Visa, relocation and remote-work results are informational text signals, not immigration advice, legal advice, employment guarantees or verification of an employer's willingness to sponsor you. Relocation openness does not establish visa sponsorship. Phrase matching can miss context or produce incorrect results. Confirm eligibility directly with the employer.

Visponsa is independently published and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by LinkedIn or the named browser vendors. Their names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
